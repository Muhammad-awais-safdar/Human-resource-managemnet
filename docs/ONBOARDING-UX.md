# Workspace Onboarding UX Documentation

## Overview

First-time tenant administrators entering a newly provisioned Awais HR workspace are guided through a structured 10-step interactive wizard and a persistent dashboard checklist widget.

---

## 10-Step Setup Wizard (`components/onboarding/OnboardingWizard.jsx`)

1. **Welcome**: Introduction to workspace setup.
2. **Company Profile & Branding**: Tenant name, subdomains, primary/secondary colors.
3. **Industry Vertical Selection**: Core HR + Vertical capability module binding.
4. **Module Configuration**: Enable Payroll, ATS, Attendance, ESS.
5. **Organization Structure**: Legal entities, cost centers, departments.
6. **Workforce & Shift Setup**: Attendance rules and shift schedules.
7. **Roles & RBAC Matrix**: Fine-grained access control settings.
8. **Integration Hub**: Slack, QuickBooks, and biometric device webhooks.
9. **Bulk Employee Import**: CSV rostering and automated invite links.
10. **Workspace Launch**: Finish setup and open dashboard.
