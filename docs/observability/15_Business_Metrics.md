# 15 - Business KPI Metrics Specification

## Financial & Subscription KPIs
- `hr_finance_mrr_usd`: Monthly Recurring Revenue ($)
- `hr_finance_arr_usd`: Annual Recurring Revenue ($)
- `hr_tenants_active`: Count of active tenant accounts
- `hr_subscriptions_seats_active`: Active billable user seats

## Operational HR KPIs
- `hr_employees_total`: Total active employee database records
- `hr_attendance_today`: Daily attendance check-ins
- `hr_payroll_failures_total`: Failed payroll processing runs requiring immediate attention
- `hr_leaves_pending`: Outstanding leave approval queue length
